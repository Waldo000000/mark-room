# Research And Competitors

## SailBoard

SailBoard is a close and useful benchmark. Publicly observable behavior indicates it offers animated Racing Rules scenarios, including Part 2 and mark-room situations, with a keyframe timeline and boat-length grid.

Important lesson from user research: SailBoard appears polished and useful, but at least some scenarios were observed to be inaccurate. MarkRoom should explicitly compete on correctness, provenance, and verification, not only interaction quality.

## Research Tasks

Early discovery should inspect:

- SailBoard scenario categories
- mobile editor behavior
- keyframe/timeline interaction
- pan and zoom
- animation
- scenario representation where observable
- how explanations cite rules
- what correctness failures can be found

Do not copy competitor content. Use competitor research to understand product behavior and quality risks.

## Naming Research

Working name: MarkRoom.

Known concern:

- `markroom.net` exists as an active Markdown viewer product using "Markroom"
- `markroom.com` was observed as registered/marketplace redirected
- no major sailing app with the exact MarkRoom/Mark Room name was found in the planning conversation

Early task:

- perform proper naming clearance across trademarks, domains, app stores, GitHub, npm, and sailing products

Do not invest heavily in final branding until this pass is complete.

## Source Research

Prioritize official and auditable sources:

- World Sailing rules
- official cases
- official interpretations
- Appendix E radio sailing material
- national authority guidance where relevant
- club training examples as secondary sources

Every source added to the corpus should have source metadata, access date, licensing notes, and extraction notes.
